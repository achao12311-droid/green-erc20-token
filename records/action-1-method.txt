Action 1 is one sheet of ordinary A4 office paper.

The sheet was not placed on a scale. This computer has no scale, so the grams are calculated from the paper size and the usual office-paper weight.

A4 size is 210 mm by 297 mm, which is 0.06237 square metres.
Ordinary office paper is 80 grams per square metre.
0.06237 x 80 = 4.9896 grams.

The contract counts whole grams, so the record stores 5 grams.
5 grams mint 0.005 ECO, because 1000 grams mint 1 ECO.
